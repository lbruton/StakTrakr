# StakTrakr v3.36.123: an easier Market tab and accessibility fixes

This release makes the Market tab easier to read and use, and fixes contrast, modals and phone layout across the app.

- The Market tab shows each dealer's premium, coloured against the other dealers, with a Configure button and clear status text.
- At phone width, Market rows turn into cards. The tab works by keyboard and with a screen reader.
- Every modal closes with Escape, keeps focus inside and stops the page from scrolling behind it.
- Text, buttons and active chips meet AA contrast in all four themes.
- Settings, Collections and the Item modals fit a phone screen. Toasts and Back to top clear the bottom nav.
