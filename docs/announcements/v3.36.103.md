# StakTrakr v3.36.103: a new Dashboard with Your stack up front

This release rebuilds the Dashboard around one Your stack card and makes it readable on every screen and theme.

- Your stack shows the stack's value, today's change, gain or loss on cost, cost basis and realized gains.
- A swap button switches the lead value between retail and melt. The ledger and PDF export follow it.
- Weight held lists each metal with its 12-month buying rate.
- The best-price ticker has a pause button and keeps every price reachable with reduced motion.
- Text meets AA contrast in all four themes, and the Dashboard works by keyboard and at phone width.
