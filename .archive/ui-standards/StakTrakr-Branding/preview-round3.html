STAKTRAKR — Round 3

No Shield — Pure S-Stack + Wordmark

Stripped back to essentials: the stacked-bar S and the wordmark. Four variations on spacing, weight, and extras.

E — Clean Stack

Balanced

Generous spacing between the S bars and wordmark. 5 bars, gold center, silver flanks at descending opacity. "Track Your Stack" tagline. Most balanced — reads well at all sizes without feeling cramped.

F — Tight Stack

Compact

S bars closer together, thin divider line separating icon from text. Extra-bold 800-weight wordmark. "Precious Metals Tracker" subtitle + "Free + Open Source" pill. More vertical info density — app store listing feel.

G — Centered Stack

Wide

Wider gold center bar stretches across more of the space. Bars are wider overall — more horizontal presence. Wordmark with generous letter-spacing. Just the tagline, nothing else. Cleanest, most confident.

H — Icon Only

PWA / Favicon

No text at all — just the S-stack at maximum size. This is the PWA icon and favicon variant. The bars fill the square. Pairs with a separate wordmark for headers/splash screens. Essential for the home screen install.
