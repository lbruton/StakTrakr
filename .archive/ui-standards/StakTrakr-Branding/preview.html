Brand Identity Exploration — March 2026

Concept 1 — Stacked Bars

Growth + Data

Ascending bars in silver and gold with a trend line connecting the peaks. Communicates portfolio growth and data tracking. The bars double as a literal "stack." Silver-dominant palette reflects the stacking community. Chart-forward — feels like a fintech app icon.

Concept 2 — Coin Stack

Classic Stacker

3D isometric stack of silver, gold, and platinum coins with an upward arrow. Most literal interpretation of "stack" — immediately recognizable to the r/Silverbugs community. Includes a tagline lockup. The 3D perspective adds depth but may be harder to simplify for small sizes.

Concept 3 — Vault Shield

Security + Trust

Shield silhouette containing stacked metal bars with a subtle blue chart line. Emphasizes security and trust — "your portfolio is protected." The gold border on dark navy feels premium. Heavy/bold wordmark below. Good for users who value the privacy-first aspect.

Concept 4 — ST Monogram

Minimal Modern

Geometric "S" built from stacked horizontal bars (like metal ingots) above a clean "T" — the bars ARE the letter. Silver-to-gold gradient flows across the bars. Most abstract and modern. Scales beautifully to favicon/16px. "Track Your Stack" tagline in a pill. Ultra-clean.
