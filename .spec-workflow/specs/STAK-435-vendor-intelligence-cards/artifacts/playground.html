Design A — Grouped Boxes

Inner boxes with bg-primary, 1px border, rounded corners — mirrors the metal card pattern exactly. Most visually consistent.

Design B — Clean Table

No inner boxes. Dashed row borders directly on the card. More compact, table-like feel. Header left-aligned.

3-column grid (coin / price / delta) with alternating row backgrounds. Portfolio stats in a single condensed line. Most information-dense.

Design D — Card within Card

Price table in a raised inner card (different bg, subtle shadow). Portfolio section flat below. Visual depth hierarchy — prices are clearly primary.
