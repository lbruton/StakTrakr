What changes from v3.36.94

  1. One card: Your stack and Totals merge into one frame with one heading. The five figures sit on top, a 1px rule separates them from the ledger, and the ledger keeps its columns, rows and click-to-open behaviour. The ledger loses its own "Totals" heading; it keeps an accessible name through a visually hidden caption ("Totals by metal").
  2. Figures, in DOM and visual order: Retail value (lead, with today's change), Melt value, Gain on cost, Cost basis, Realized. The four figures after the lead share the rest of the row equally.
  3. Sub-lines: Melt keeps ounces and Items. Gain reads "+13.4% at retail", since Cost basis has its own figure. Cost basis shows the average cost per ounce (the ledger's All Metals Avg cost/oz). Realized shows its percentage on the cost of the Items that left the stack, the same figure as the ledger cell.
  4. Realized hides with the Show Realized setting, in the headline and in the ledger. The other three figures widen to fill the row.
  5. Order: spot cards, Your stack, Best Price Ticker. Saved layouts keep the user's order; the two old sections become one entry at the earlier position.
  6. No Items: the card shows the title, "No Items yet." and Add Item. The ledger hides, since it has no row to show.
  7. Phone: the lead takes the full width, the other four figures share two columns, and the ledger keeps its two-line blocks under the rule.
  8. Demo data: the seed inventory plus one sold Silver Item, so Realized shows a value. Retail equals melt because no seed Item has a retail price.
