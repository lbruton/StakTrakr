What STRK-505 changes from v3.36.95

  1. Lead swap: a round button next to the lead label swaps Retail value and Melt value. Its accessible name names the action ("Lead with melt value"). The 26px button has a 44px hit area. The DOM order follows the lead, so the reading order matches the screen.
  2. Today's change sits under the lead. Under Retail lead it reads "today at spot", since the change is a melt change. Under Melt lead it reads "today".
  3. Gain on cost follows the lead: retail minus cost basis, or melt minus cost basis. The sub-line names the basis ("+17.9% at retail", "+13.4% at melt").
  4. The second figure gets a sub-line of its own. Melt reads "8 Items, priced at spot", with no summed ounces. Retail reads "+4.0% over melt", the premium the dealers price in.
  5. Weight held: a column to the right of the figures with one row per held metal in Metal Order. Each row has the metal mark, the name, the ounces (the ledger's Weight (oz)) and the 12-month pace from the breakdown modal's 1Y strip. A metal with no buys in 12 months reads "No buys".
  6. Activity line under the figures: invested, acquisitions and market for the All Metals scope over the last 12 months, the same figures as the modal's 1Y strip.
  7. Phone: the weights list and the activity line wrap below the figures. The weights list sits under a 1px rule.
  8. Open question: the ledger's Gain column stays retail-based under Melt lead. The headline then shows melt gain over a ledger that shows retail gain.
  9. Demo data: the seed inventory with dealer prices added to three metals, so retail and melt differ. Palladium has no retail price and falls back to melt. Activity and pace figures are illustrative.

STRK-500 (v3.36.95) from v3.36.94

  1. One card: Your stack and Totals merge into one frame with one heading. The five figures sit on top, a 1px rule separates them from the ledger, and the ledger keeps its columns, rows and click-to-open behaviour. The ledger loses its own "Totals" heading; it keeps an accessible name through a visually hidden caption ("Totals by metal").
  2. Figures, in DOM and visual order: Retail value (lead, with today's change), Melt value, Gain on cost, Cost basis, Realized. The four figures after the lead share the rest of the row equally.
  3. Sub-lines: Melt keeps ounces and Items. Gain reads "+13.4% at retail", since Cost basis has its own figure. Cost basis shows the average cost per ounce (the ledger's All Metals Avg cost/oz). Realized shows its percentage on the cost of the Items that left the stack, the same figure as the ledger cell.
  4. Realized hides with the Show Realized setting, in the headline and in the ledger. The other three figures widen to fill the row.
  5. Order: spot cards, Your stack, Best Price Ticker. Saved layouts keep the user's order; the two old sections become one entry at the earlier position.
  6. No Items: the card shows the title, "No Items yet." and Add Item. The ledger hides, since it has no row to show.
  7. Phone: the lead takes the full width, the other four figures share two columns, and the ledger keeps its two-line blocks under the rule.
  8. Demo data: the seed inventory plus one sold Silver Item, so Realized shows a value.
