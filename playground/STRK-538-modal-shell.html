STRK-538 modal shell

The shell

One header: Instrument Serif 400 title on the left, one 44px close button on the right, a 1px rule under it. The body scrolls; the header and footer stay put. The footer puts the primary action on the right with a neutral outlined Cancel beside it. An extra action (Clear log, Delete) sits on the left. The card is opaque on an opaque overlay: no backdrop blur on either layer. The header has no gradient or tinted fill.
