STAKTRAKR — Round 2

Shield + S-Stack Monogram Variants

Direction: Shield shape containing the stacked-bar S monogram. Silver-dominant bars with a gold center accent. Exploring shield weight, bar spacing, detail level, and wordmark placement.

A — Shield Monogram

Detailed

Full shield with silver-to-gold gradient across all S bars. Subtle blue chart line weaves through the S shape. Inner shield outline adds depth. 7 bars for a detailed S. Most ornate — conveys complexity and sophistication.

B — Shield Compact

Badge

Outlined shield (no fill) with silver bars + one gold center bar. The gold bar pops as the focal point. Tiny blue trend dots in the top-right corner hint at data tracking. Badge-like feel — like a quality mark or certification stamp.

C — Shield Filled

Premium

Dark filled shield with gold glow on the top edge — the gold light source draws the eye upward. S bars are large and prominent. Gold dot at the shield's bottom point. Extra-bold wordmark. The most premium/luxury feel of the four.

D — Shield Minimal

Clean

Ultra-thin shield outline (barely there) with just 5 thick bars — fewer elements, more graphic impact. Larger wordmark sits inside the lower shield area. "Track Your Stack" tagline + "Free + Open Source" pill. Community-friendly tone. Best at small sizes.
